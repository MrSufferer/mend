# Mend

Mend recovers paid document-processing orders whose delivery remains unresolved.

## Language

**Delivery deadline**:
The end of the delivery period purchased with an order, measured from confirmed payment. Delayed investigation does not extend it.

**Overdue order**:
An order whose delivery deadline has passed without confirmed delivery. Being overdue alone does not establish entitlement to a refund.

**Refund entitlement**:
An authorization for the original payer to receive a specified refund for an eligible failed order. It is distinct from a requested or completed refund.


**Delivery**:
Authenticated availability of a verified downloadable result to the paying customer, whether or not they download it.

**Late delivery**:
Delivery established after the purchased delivery deadline, including restoration of previously inaccessible output.

**File expiry**:
The end of a private file’s retention period. It is separate from an order’s delivery or refund outcome and does not resolve an outstanding obligation.


**Escalated recovery case**:
An unresolved recovery case requiring operator review because evidence or agent execution is insufficient. Escalation is not a delivery or refund outcome.
